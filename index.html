FAQ

All guests will be provided a room at the château. We may need to arrange roommates for some guests, and will coordinate with you in advance if so.

If you prefer other lodging, the nearby village has several options.

The château has its own restaurant, professional chef, and garden. Food will be prepared on-site using fresh ingredients from the property. All meals will be vegetarian.

The château is approximately an hour by car from Paris. You're responsible for arranging your own transport, but we'll do what we can to help coordinate.

If you're renting a car, let us know so we can arrange a carpool spreadsheet.

We've limited the guest list to those directly invited. Due to space constraints at the château, we're unable to accommodate plus ones.

Anya and Matt will be in Paris from Wednesday, October 7, staying at hotel Artemisia Montmartre, a short walk away from a major metro stop and in Paris' arts district. The hotel is ~$250 per night and has great common areas to hang out.

You're welcome to book a room at the same hotel (or nearby) and meet up with us for sightseeing before the wedding! This is optional and separate from the wedding weekend.

No gifts are expected or necessary. If you'd like to give something, we have a small registry [Coming Soon].

Friday (Cocktail Attire): Elegant but less formal than black tie. Cocktail dresses, blazers with dress pants.

Saturday (Black Tie Optional): Tuxedos, long dresses, dark suits. We welcome non-American formal attire.
