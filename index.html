Things to Know

Accommodations

All guests will be provided a room at the château. We may need to place some guests with roommates, but will reach out to you in advance before doing so.

Alternative: If you prefer separate lodging, guests are free to book accommodations in the nearby village.

Transportation

The château is approximately 50 minutes by car from Paris. While guests are responsible for their own transport, we will do our best to help.

Carpooling: Please let us know if you are renting a car so we can arrange a carpool spreadsheet.

Plus Ones

After careful consideration, unfortunately we won't be able to accommodate plus ones. We have directly invited all attendees. While we'd love for everyone to bring their partners, we'd quickly run out of space at the château.

Thank you for your understanding!
